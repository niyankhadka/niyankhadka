Eight years in, working across custom WordPress, system architecture, digital marketing and automation.

I help businesses turn ideas into reliable digital products: high performance websites and bespoke WordPress platforms, SaaS architecture, CRM pipelines, automation workflows, and growth campaigns you can actually measure.

I work from Kathmandu, a few hours ahead of most of my clients, and several centuries from the offices they sit in.

## How I work

> No surprises. That is the whole method.

<table>
<tr>
<td width="25%" valign="top">

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/01-F0883E?style=flat-square&labelColor=161B22" />

#### Understand

Before writing code, I clarify who visits, what they need to do, and what success means in measurable terms.

<sub>**You get:** a written brief, agreed before anything is built.</sub>

</td>
<td width="25%" valign="top">

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/02-F0883E?style=flat-square&labelColor=161B22" />

#### Architect

Data model, templates, components, integrations, stack, and the places where the system may eventually strain.

<sub>**You get:** a structure diagram and a decided technical direction.</sub>

</td>
<td width="25%" valign="top">

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/03-F0883E?style=flat-square&labelColor=161B22" />

#### Build

Clean, documented, version controlled, and tested across the devices your visitors actually use.

<sub>**You get:** a working, tested, version controlled product.</sub>

</td>
<td width="25%" valign="top">

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/04-F0883E?style=flat-square&labelColor=161B22" />

#### Hand over

The finished system should be usable by your team, not only by the person who built it.

<sub>**You get:** documentation, a walkthrough, and ongoing support.</sub>

</td>
</tr>
